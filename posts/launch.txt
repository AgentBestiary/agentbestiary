## Post 2 — what makes it different (reply in thread)

Most agent-gated drops ask you to trust a server. This one doesn't:

- the handle on the piece is read from the Agent ID registry at mint time
- the image is SVG generated by the contract
- reveal is commit-reveal: anyone holding the secret can trigger it
- no owner, no pause, no withdraw

You can read all of it on BscScan before you spend a wei.
